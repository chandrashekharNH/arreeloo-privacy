The short version: ARreeloo does its work on your iPhone or iPad. We do not collect your videos, photos, voice or any personal information. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Who we are

ARreeloo is an app for recording and editing videos, made by Chandrashekhar N H ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Information we collect

None. We do not run servers that receive anything from the app, so we never see or store:

  • your videos, photos or voice recordings
  • your name, email address or contacts
  • your location
  • device identifiers, usage data or crash reports

What the app uses on your device

ARreeloo asks for permission before using each of these, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings.

  • CameraTo record your videos and to add live effects to the room around you.
  • MicrophoneSo your videos have sound, and to record a voice-over.
  • PhotosTo let you pick videos and photos to edit, and to save your finished videos.
  • Speech recognitionTo turn a voice-over you record into captions.

Everything you record, pick or edit stays on your device. Finished videos are saved to your Photos library, where you decide whether to share them.

Captions and Apple's speech recognition

Captions are made with Apple's speech recognition. For languages your device can handle on its own, the recording never leaves your device. For other languages, Apple's speech recognition sends the voice-over audio to Apple to be turned into text. That audio goes to Apple, not to us, and is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy. If you do not use captions, nothing is sent.

Purchases

ARreeloo offers an optional one-time purchase that removes the watermark from your videos. The purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information. The app only learns from your device whether the purchase has been made.

Tracking and advertising

ARreeloo does not track you across apps or websites, does not show ads, and does not include any third-party analytics or advertising software.

Sharing your information

Because we do not collect your information, we have nothing to share or sell, and we do not do either.

Deleting your data

Your content lives only on your device. Deleting a video in the app or in Photos removes it, and deleting the app removes everything the app stored.

Children

ARreeloo does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
